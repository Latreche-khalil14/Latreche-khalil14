I am a **Computer Science graduate** from the **University of 20 August 1955 – Skikda**, ranked in the **top 6% of my cohort** in **Information Systems & Software Engineering (ISIL)**.

My engineering work focuses on solving the fundamental bottlenecks of enterprise AI:
1. **Explainability over Opacity:** Eliminating black-box LLM decisions through **SHAP**, **LIME**, and real-time step-by-step reasoning transparency.
2. **Privacy-Preserving On-Premise AI:** Building local, air-gapped RAG pipelines utilizing **Ollama (Qwen2.5)** and **FAISS** to guarantee complete data sovereignty for corporate compliance.
3. **Autonomous Agentic Reliability:** Moving beyond fragile prompt chains to robust **multi-agent architectures** with parallel task decomposition, architectural PII sanitization, and intent drift detection.
